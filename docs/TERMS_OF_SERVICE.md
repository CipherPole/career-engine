## 2. Platform Purpose & Scope of Services

Career Engine is an artificial intelligence-augmented engineering career intelligence and technical skill benchmarking platform designed to help software engineers, platform engineers, DevOps leads, and technology professionals evaluate and grow their capabilities.

The Platform provides:
- **ATS Resume Keyword Gap Analysis:** Algorithms that match resume content against target engineering job descriptions.
- **Interactive Multi-Dimensional Skills Radar Mapping:** Visual benchmarks comparing user proficiencies against industry percentiles.
- **Job Application Telemetry & Tracker:** Isolated tracking tools for monitoring interviews, stages, and compensation targets.
- **Training & Certification Roadmaps:** Structured paths for closing technical skill gaps.
